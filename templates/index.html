FEATURES — WHAT'S IN THE RECORD

Built for the five minutes after the meeting.

Every feature exists to remove one piece of post-meeting busywork.

✓

Speaker-labeled transcripts

Every line is attributed automatically, even in crosstalk-heavy calls with five or more voices.

◆

Decision extraction

The system flags the moment a decision is actually made, not just discussed, and logs who made it.

→

Action items, assigned

Owner and task are pulled from context and routed straight to the right person's inbox or board.

⌕

Searchable archive

Every past meeting is indexed by what was said, not just when — search "pricing" across a year of calls.

⇄

Native integrations

Notes land in Notion, tasks in Asana, summaries in Slack — no copy-paste, no separate export step.

⚿

Private by default

Recordings are encrypted end to end and never used to train shared models. You control retention.
